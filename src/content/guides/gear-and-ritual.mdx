Gear and Ritual systems are part of Bomb Farm's long-term progression layer. This guide explains the confirmed upgrade basics, how to approach spending carefully, and which item details still need a current in-game check.

## Gear and Ritual basics

Official Steam copy verifies:

- Gear can drop sealed and be upgraded.
- Equipment upgrades run from +0 to +15.
- Duplicate items can be fused to increase stars.
- Gems and Ritual are part of character improvement.
- Rare items can be traded on the Steam Community Market.

Steam achievements add milestone support: Steady Hammer references forging gear to +8, Masterpiece references forging gear to +15, Relic references obtaining Mythic gear, and End of the Line references taking a gear piece to level 300.

## How to use this page

Treat gear and Ritual decisions as long-term progression choices. Before spending scarce resources, compare the current game client against the latest Steam announcements, especially when update notes mention chests, Ritual behavior, item inspection, rarity frames, or Market validation.

Use related pages for context:

- [Progression](/guides/progression/) connects gear milestones to the campaign frame.
- [Achievements](/tools/achievements/) lists the Steam milestone language.
- [Steam Market Safety](/guides/steam-market/) explains why tradable-item decisions should stay separate from price advice.

## Market boundary

The store page says rare items can be traded on the Steam Community Market, and the EULA says Steam Market prices are player transactions rather than values set, endorsed, or guaranteed by the developer. Keep gear decisions separate from investment-style claims.

## Details to check in game

Do not rely on item tables, rarity odds, forge odds, upgrade costs, Market values, profit advice, item-price history, or best-in-slot rankings unless they have a current source.
