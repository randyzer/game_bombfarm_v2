Bomb Farm combines idle RPG pacing with short decision loops. Steam describes a bomber squad gathering resources, destroying rocks, searching for rare items, upgrading equipment, using a large skill tree, and trading rare items on the Steam Community Market.

## The verified core loop

The safest way to understand Bomb Farm is as a loop with four visible parts:

1. Push the mine through auto battles and stage progress.
2. Use rewards to improve heroes, gear, rituals, gems, and skill tree growth.
3. Rotate between three teams so another team can keep progress moving.
4. Recheck official updates before acting on codes, rewards, Market behavior, or patch-specific systems.

That loop is enough to help a new player avoid the biggest trap: chasing a "best" answer before knowing which system the current session is actually testing.

## What to watch while a run is active

Look for the current stage, whether the active team is still progressing, whether rewards created an upgrade opportunity, and whether a different team can continue the mine. Steam verifies the three-team concept, but this page does not claim exact energy recovery or optimal rotation timers.

## What to handle between runs

Between pushes, check systems that persist:

- [Hero Roles](/heroes/roles/) to understand the official role labels.
- [Gear and Ritual](/guides/gear-and-ritual/) for safe upgrade boundaries.
- [Skill Tree](/guides/skill-tree/) for account-level progression without fake node math.
- [Achievements](/tools/achievements/) for long-term milestones such as difficulty clears, gear upgrades, Homes, and Stage 600.

## Market awareness

Bomb Farm supports Steam Market item trading. That makes safety, account hygiene, and trade confirmation important, but prices and profit advice can become stale quickly. Use [Steam Market Safety](/guides/steam-market/) before selling or moving items.

## Patch-sensitive habits

When an official update mentions rewards, stashes, XP, Market rules, Ritual behavior, or codes, revisit the related guide before acting. The [Latest Update](/updates/latest/) page records the newest official item found during the last freshness check.
