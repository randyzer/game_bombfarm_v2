Bomb Farm supports Steam Market item trading. Use this page to avoid risky trades, read Steam prompts carefully, and separate item safety from price speculation.

## Trading basics

Steam's Bomb Farm store page says rare items can be traded with other players on the Steam Community Market. The Bomb Farm EULA adds important boundaries: virtual items are licensed, not property; Steam Market prices come from player transactions; the developer does not set, endorse, or guarantee those prices; and items can be modified, suspended, removed, reset, or revoked.

Steam support pages also describe Market and trading restrictions, including holds, listing restrictions, and Steam Guard-related delays. Because those rules depend on account state and platform policy, always check Steam's current prompts before confirming a listing or trade.

## Safety first

Use Steam's official Market interface, review item names and quantities before confirming, and be careful with links or messages that ask you to trade outside normal Steam flows. Do not treat outside messages, off-platform payment requests, or "guaranteed value" claims as safe.

## What players can rely on

Bomb Farm has Market-tradable items on Steam, and official updates have mentioned Market inventory, inspection, validation, and message improvements. On September 4, 2026, the latest official update check found the September 3 announcement mentioning clearer Market inventories and item inspection before sale.

Use [Latest Update](/updates/latest/) for the newest official announcement summary and [Codes](/guides/codes/) when a reward or code note might affect inventory behavior.

## What to check before selling

- Confirm the item is the one you intend to move.
- Read any Steam hold or restriction message before listing.
- Keep account security current, especially Steam Guard prompts.
- Recheck Bomb Farm announcements if Market eligibility or item behavior changed recently.
- Avoid external payment, gift card, or wallet-credit trade offers.

## Prices and economy

Current values, price history, arbitrage routes, profit claims, and automated price tracking are not covered here. Check Steam directly before any Market action.
