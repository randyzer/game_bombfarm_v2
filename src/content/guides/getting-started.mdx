Bomb Farm is a Steam free-to-play idle RPG where a bomber squad mines resources, destroys rocks, upgrades persistent systems, and returns to the mine with stronger heroes and equipment. This first-session guide gives you a clean starting route without pretending every hidden value is known.

## Quick facts before you play

- Steam lists Bomb Farm as released on August 28, 2026.
- The store page describes idle progression, auto battles, heroes, equipment, a skill tree, gems, rituals, and Steam Market item trading.
- The official campaign frame is five worlds, five difficulties, and 600 stages.
- Steam lists 21 achievements.
- Some facts are patch-sensitive, especially codes, rewards, Market behavior, and update-specific systems.

## First-session route

Start by learning the game as a loop, not as a spreadsheet. Push stages while the run is moving, then spend downtime checking the systems that persist after a run: heroes, gear, rituals, gems, and the skill tree. The Steam page verifies that players can switch between three teams, so do not judge progress from one active group alone.

## Account and platform notes

Steam lists Bomb Farm with single-player support, Steam achievements, Steam Cloud, in-app purchases, and Family Sharing. The store page also references third-party BombFarm account linking. Treat account, inventory, and Market actions as platform-sensitive: use official Steam flows and keep your Steam security settings current.

## What not to assume

Leave exact drop tables, current prices, best squads, hero rankings, skill-tree values, and item profit advice out of your early decisions unless the claim comes with a clear official or direct verification source.
