Bomb Farm progression appears across several systems rather than one linear checklist. Start with the confirmed campaign structure and Steam achievement milestones, then check the current game before trusting exact optimization advice.

## Campaign frame

Steam verifies five worlds, five difficulties, and 600 stages. The Steam achievement list also includes difficulty-clear achievements from Easy through Inferno, with Inferno Cleared described as reaching stage 600 and clearing the campaign.

That does not mean this page can claim the fastest path to stage 600. It means the public roadmap can safely treat world, difficulty, and stage progress as core long-term goals.

## Systems that carry progress

Use the store page and achievements together to decide where to spend attention:

- Heroes and roles shape team structure, but a reliable roster or tier list needs current hero data.
- Equipment upgrades are verified up to +15 through the store and achievement language.
- Ritual and hero-star milestones appear in achievements, but exact odds and formulas are not published.
- The skill tree is verified as a large account progression system, but node values and build orders are deferred.
- Homes appear in achievements, including Home level 20 and all five Homes, but this page does not confirm the detailed Home rules.

## Early planning

Prioritize systems that persist across runs, then use [Achievements](/tools/achievements/) as a milestone checklist. If an update changes rewards, XP, Market behavior, or stash limits, review [Latest Update](/updates/latest/) before treating old progression advice as current.

## Details to check in game

Do not rely on exact XP rates, drop tables, best upgrades, stage walls, time-to-clear estimates, payback calculations, or best-team recommendations unless they come from a current source.
